Who We Are

A Universe Built for You

Alice Shoe Niverse is more than a shoe store — it's a destination. We believe footwear is deeply personal; it's an extension of who you are, how you feel, and where you're going.

Founded with the vision of making premium, stylish footwear accessible to everyone, we've curated a collection that spans every style, occasion, and personality. From boardroom to dance floor, we've got your sole.

Our team personally selects every pair, ensuring each style meets our standards for quality, comfort, and character. When you shop with us, you're not just buying shoes — you're stepping into your own universe.
